Questions

What is materials science?

The study of how a material's structure — from atomic bonds to grain boundaries — determines its properties and performance. It bridges physics, chemistry, and engineering.

Is this really free?

Yes. Community-driven, no paywall, no data selling. We keep the lights on through sponsored listings from equipment vendors and software companies — clearly labeled, never affecting content.

How is AI changing materials discovery?

ML models trained on materials databases predict properties of compounds never synthesized. Google DeepMind's GNoME found 2.2 million new stable crystals. The bottleneck shifted from "what to try" to "how fast can we make it."

What is the strongest material?

Depends on "strong." Graphene: highest tensile strength (~130 GPa). Diamond: hardest. Spider silk: best toughness-to-weight. Tungsten: highest melting point. No single winner — every material is a set of trade-offs.

Structure determines properties. Properties determine performance. Performance determines whether the thing you built survives contact with reality. That is materials science.
